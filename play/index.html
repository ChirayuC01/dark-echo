RESONANCE

You are blind in the dark.
Sound is the only light.
Every step echoes. Every echo betrays you.

WASD / arrows · SPACE = pulse · P / ESC = pause

How to Play

The screen is black. You see only what sound reveals. Make a sound and the nearby walls light up as the echo passes — then find the hidden exit and reach it. But everything you hear can hear you too. Move loud to see far; move quiet to stay alive.

The color language

White — youYour footsteps, pulse and echoes. Your feet leave white prints. Everything white is sound you made.
Blue — waterSlows you down and makes your steps louder. Harder to stay quiet.
Yellow — the way outThe exit, plus keys, doors and switches. The exit is a pulsing yellow beacon, hidden until your sound finds it. A switch that ripples can be opened by sound — clap at it from a distance.
Red — dangerEnemies, hazards and crushers. Touching red means death.

What hunts you

PatrolWalks a fixed route — predictable. Reacts to loud sounds. Time your crossing when it looks away.
ChaserWanders until it hears you, then rushes the sound. Its ring flares brighter while hunting.
SentrySweeps a cone of vision. It detects you by sight, not sound — cross behind it. A pulse stuns it.
Blind StalkerHears everything — even crouched steps. Fast and relentless, but loses you quickly if you go silent.
HazardStationary. Emits scan pulses that give away its position. Lethal if you touch it — keep your distance.
ScreamerA silent trap. Any sound that reaches it — even a stray echo — triggers a scream that alerts every enemy nearby.
CrusherA heavy slab sliding back and forth. Slip through between passes — it crushes on contact.

Remember

  • Louder means you see more — and so do they. Pulse only when you must.
  • Standing still makes no sound. Stop to listen and let a threat pass.
  • Water and screamers make noise for you — cross them carefully.
